## 4. Mapping
Error as documented in the private note: Random oversampling (minority rows copied with replacement until the classes have equal counts) is applied to all 400 records; stratified 5-fold cross-validation with an unpruned CART tree then runs on the enlarged data; sensitivity for the positive class, pooled over the test folds (which contain copies), is the statistic. Claim holds when sensitivity >= 0.60.

Correction as documented: Stratified 5-fold cross-validation on the original 400 records; random oversampling of the training folds only; sensitivity on the original test-fold records, pooled.

Model-family choice in the private note: Unpruned CART decision tree with random oversampling. Chosen by me: an unpruned tree reproduces a copied row exactly, so the copy leak is visible; the abstract names no classifier.

Lines of `faulty_body.py` that carry the error (parameters are those of the private note, where the source gives none the brief author's choice; the source's own sizes and bias figures are in the private note's `sizes_note`):
   - faulty_body.py line 126, `xs, ys = _oversample(x, y, rng)`: random oversampling of all records before any fold is formed.
   - faulty_body.py line 127, `folds = _stratified_folds(rng, ys, 5)`: folds drawn on the enlarged data, so copies are test rows.
   - faulty_body.py line 128, `ye = ys`: sensitivity scored on the enlarged labels.

Lines of `fixed_body.py` that carry the correction:
   - fixed_body.py line 126, `folds = _stratified_folds(rng, y, 5)`: folds drawn on the original records.
   - fixed_body.py line 131, `xs, ys = _oversample(x[~te], y[~te], rng)`: oversampling of the training part of each fold only.
   - fixed_body.py line 127, `ye = y`: scored on the original test-fold records.

The remaining lines (helpers, guards, statistic and threshold line) are identical in the two files. `criterion` occupies
lines 112 to 133 of fixed_body.py.

Implementation choices the private note leaves open (identical in both files): Stratified folds. Oversampling: original rows plus rows of the smaller class drawn with replacement until equal counts. Unpruned CART (no depth limit, minimum leaf 1).

**Estimand.** Faulty: sensitivity on test rows that are copies of training rows. Fixed: sensitivity on new records.
