## 3. Brief
- Claim: The monthly environmental measurements predict the abundance in later months of the series: the out-of-sample R-squared is at least 0.30.
- Data keys:
   - `abundance` float, (months,): monthly abundance index of the species, on a log scale, in time order
   - `environment` float, (months, variables): monthly environmental measurements, in time order
- Sizes: months 600, variables 10.

## 4. Mapping
Error as documented in the private note: 10-fold cross-validation with months assigned to folds uniformly at random; a random forest (50 trees, min leaf size 5) predicts abundance from the month's 10 environmental variables; R-squared = 1 - SSE/SST from the pooled held-out predictions. Claim holds when R-squared >= 0.30.

Correction as documented: Forward-in-time blocked evaluation: the 600 months are divided into 10 consecutive blocks; for blocks 3 to 10, the forest is fitted on the months of all earlier blocks only and predicts the block; R-squared is computed from the pooled block predictions (the held-out blocks' own mean as the baseline).

Model-family choice in the private note: Random forest regression (50 trees, min leaf 5). Chosen by me: ROB17's abstract names no model; a flexible non-parametric model is the setting in which interpolation between neighbouring months is easy.

Lines of `faulty_body.py` that carry the error (parameters are those of the private note, where the source gives none the brief author's choice; the source's own sizes and bias figures are in the private note's `sizes_note`):
   - faulty_body.py line 115, `folds = _random_folds(rng, n, 10)`: months assigned to 10 folds uniformly at random.
   - faulty_body.py line 120, `ye = y`: R-squared over all months.

Lines of `fixed_body.py` that carry the correction:
   - fixed_body.py line 115, `edges = (np.arange(11) * n) // 10`: 10 consecutive blocks.
   - fixed_body.py line 119, `te = np.arange(edges[b], edges[b + 1])`: blocks 3 to 10 predicted from all earlier blocks only.
   - fixed_body.py line 122, `ye = y[edges[2]:]`: baseline = mean of the predicted blocks.

The remaining lines (helpers, guards, statistic and threshold line) are identical in the two files. `criterion` occupies
lines 101 to 125 of fixed_body.py.

Implementation choices the private note leaves open (identical in both files): Random forest: 50 trees on bootstrap resamples, minimum leaf 5, a third of the variables searched per node (3 of 10), variance reduction. 10 uniformly random folds. Blocks of n/10 months (60).

**Estimand.** Faulty: skill at predicting months whose neighbours in time were used for fitting. Fixed: skill at predicting later months from earlier ones.
