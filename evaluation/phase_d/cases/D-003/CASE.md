## 4. Mapping
Error as documented in the private note: Random oversampling (minority rows copied with replacement until classes have equal counts) is applied to all 1000 records; stratified 10-fold cross-validation with a 5-nearest-neighbour classifier (probability = share of positive neighbours) then runs on the enlarged data; the AUC of the pooled test-fold scores is the statistic. Claim holds when AUC >= 0.75.

Correction as documented: Stratified 10-fold cross-validation on the original records; oversampling of the training folds only; AUC on the original test-fold records, pooled.

Model-family choice in the private note: 5-nearest-neighbour classifier with random oversampling. Chosen by me: the abstract names no classifier; duplicates are their own nearest neighbours.

Lines of `faulty_body.py` that carry the error (parameters are those of the private note, where the source gives none the brief author's choice; the source's own sizes and bias figures are in the private note's `sizes_note`):
   - faulty_body.py line 78, `xs, ys = _oversample(x, y, rng)`: random oversampling of all records before any fold is formed.
   - faulty_body.py line 79, `folds = _stratified_folds(rng, ys, 10)`: folds drawn on the enlarged data, so copies are test rows.
   - faulty_body.py line 80, `ye = ys`: AUC scored on the enlarged labels.

Lines of `fixed_body.py` that carry the correction:
   - fixed_body.py line 78, `folds = _stratified_folds(rng, y, 10)`: folds drawn on the original records.
   - fixed_body.py line 83, `xs, ys = _oversample(x[~te], y[~te], rng)`: oversampling of the training part of each fold only.
   - fixed_body.py line 79, `ye = y`: AUC scored on the original test-fold records.

The remaining lines (helpers, guards, statistic and threshold line) are identical in the two files. `criterion` occupies
lines 64 to 85 of fixed_body.py.

Implementation choices the private note leaves open (identical in both files): Stratified folds. Oversampling as in B2B-05. 5-NN on standardised columns (the brief does not say standardised); probability = share of positive neighbours; AUC with average ranks for ties.

**Estimand.** Faulty: AUC on test rows that are copies of training rows. Fixed: AUC on new records.
