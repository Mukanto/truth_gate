## 3. Brief
- Claim: The daily driver measurements predict the daily river level in later days of the record: the root-mean-square error of the prediction is at most 0.70 standard deviations of the level.
- Data keys:
   - `level` float, (days,): daily river level, in time order
   - `drivers` float, (days, variables): daily measurements of the variables that may influence the river level, in time order
- Sizes: days 1500, variables 6.

## 4. Mapping
Error as documented in the private note: 10-fold cross-validation with days assigned to folds uniformly at random; a 5-nearest-neighbour regression on the standardised driver variables predicts the level; RMSE of the pooled held-out predictions, divided by the standard deviation of the level, is the statistic. Claim holds when this ratio <= 0.70.

Correction as documented: Forward-in-time blocked evaluation: 1500 days divided into 10 consecutive blocks; for blocks 3 to 10, the regression is fitted on the days of all earlier blocks only and predicts the block; pooled RMSE divided by the standard deviation of the level in the predicted blocks.

Model-family choice in the private note: 5-nearest-neighbour regression on standardised drivers. Chosen by me: the abstract names no model; k-NN is cheap and its error on interpolation is easy to see.

Lines of `faulty_body.py` that carry the error (parameters are those of the private note, where the source gives none the brief author's choice; the source's own sizes and bias figures are in the private note's `sizes_note`):
   - faulty_body.py line 41, `folds = _random_folds(rng, n, 10)`: days assigned to 10 folds uniformly at random.
   - faulty_body.py line 46, `ye = y`: scaled by the SD of the level over all days.

Lines of `fixed_body.py` that carry the correction:
   - fixed_body.py line 41, `edges = (np.arange(11) * n) // 10`: 10 consecutive blocks.
   - fixed_body.py line 45, `te = np.arange(edges[b], edges[b + 1])`: blocks 3 to 10 predicted from all earlier days only.
   - fixed_body.py line 48, `ye = y[edges[2]:]`: scaled by the SD of the level in the predicted blocks.

The remaining lines (helpers, guards, statistic and threshold line) are identical in the two files. `criterion` occupies
lines 27 to 51 of fixed_body.py.

Implementation choices the private note leaves open (identical in both files): 5-NN regression on drivers standardised with the fitted part; RMSE over the evaluated days divided by their population SD (ddof 0). 10 uniformly random folds in the faulty file.

**Estimand.** Faulty: error on days whose neighbouring days were used for fitting. Fixed: error on later days predicted from earlier ones, relative to the spread of the level.
