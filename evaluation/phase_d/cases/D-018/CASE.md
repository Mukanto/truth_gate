## 4. Mapping
Error as documented in the private note: Record-wise 10-fold cross-validation: all 16 x 60 = 960 recordings are assigned to folds uniformly at random, each carrying its subject's disease label; a random forest (50 trees) scores each recording; the AUC of the pooled held-out scores is the statistic. Claim holds when AUC >= 0.70.

Correction as documented: Subject-wise cross-validation: all recordings of a subject are in the same fold (leave-two-subjects-out style, 8 folds); same forest; AUC of pooled held-out recording scores.

Model-family choice in the private note: Random forest, 50 trees, as in the source's simulation setting (a disease effect, cross-subject and within-subject variability).
