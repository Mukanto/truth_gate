## 4. Mapping
Error as documented in the private note: All 72 samples rank genes by absolute two-sample t-statistic; the top 20 are kept; 10-fold cross-validation of a nearest-centroid classifier on those 20 genes runs on all samples; AUC of the pooled held-out scores (difference of distances to the two centroids) is the statistic. Claim holds when AUC >= 0.90.

Correction as documented: In each fold, genes are ranked and the top 20 chosen using the training samples only; centroids from those; AUC on the pooled held-out scores.

Model-family choice in the private note: t-ranking, top 20 genes, nearest-centroid classifier. Chosen by me: simple and cheap; the source's own are SVM-RFE and Fisher LDA, which are costlier at 7129 genes.

Lines of `faulty_body.py` that carry the error (parameters are those of the private note, where the source gives none the brief author's choice; the source's own sizes and bias figures are in the private note's `sizes_note`):
   - faulty_body.py line 70, `sel = _top_genes(x, y, 20)`: top 20 genes by |t| chosen once on all samples, before the cross-validation.
   - faulty_body.py line 76, `scores[te] = _centroid_scores(xs[~te], y[~te], xs[te])`: the cross-validation then runs on those pre-selected genes.

Lines of `fixed_body.py` that carry the correction:
   - fixed_body.py line 74, `sel = _top_genes(x[~te], y[~te], 20)`: top 20 genes chosen inside each fold, on the training samples only.
   - fixed_body.py line 75, `scores[te] = _centroid_scores(x[~te][:, sel], y[~te], x[te][:, sel])`: centroids from those genes, held-out scores.

The remaining lines (helpers, guards, statistic and threshold line) are identical in the two files. `criterion` occupies
lines 55 to 76 of fixed_body.py.

Implementation choices the private note leaves open (identical in both files): Stratified folds. t-statistic with pooled variance, top 20; nearest centroid on the raw expression of the selected genes, score = distance to the False-class centroid minus distance to the True-class centroid.

**Estimand.** Faulty: AUC of a rule whose genes were chosen with the test samples' labels. Fixed: AUC of the whole procedure on new samples.
