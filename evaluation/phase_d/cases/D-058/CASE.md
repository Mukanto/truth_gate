## 3. Brief
- Claim: Features computed from wearable-sensor signals recognise walking in newly recruited subjects: the classification error rate for walking versus other activities is at most 0.10.
- Data keys:
   - `signal_features` float, (subjects, windows, features): features computed from the sensor signal in each time window of each subject
   - `walking` bool, (subjects, windows): True if the subject was walking during the window
- Sizes: subjects 30, windows 30, features 40.

## 4. Mapping
Error as documented in the private note: Record-wise 10-fold cross-validation: the 900 windows (30 subjects x 30 windows) are assigned to folds uniformly at random; a random forest (50 trees) classifies walking from the 40 features; the error rate pooled over folds is the statistic. Claim holds when error <= 0.10.

Correction as documented: Subject-wise 10-fold cross-validation: all windows of a subject are in the same fold (3 subjects per fold); same forest and statistic.

Model-family choice in the private note: Random forest, 50 trees: the source's classifier (activity recognition, tuned on out-of-bag error; I fix the settings).

Lines of `faulty_body.py` that carry the error (parameters are those of the private note, where the source gives none the brief author's choice; the source's own sizes and bias figures are in the private note's `sizes_note`):
   - faulty_body.py line 116, `folds = _random_folds(rng, n, 10)`: windows assigned to 10 folds uniformly at random (record-wise).

Lines of `fixed_body.py` that carry the correction:
   - fixed_body.py line 116, `folds = np.repeat(_random_folds(rng, s, 10), m)`: subjects assigned to 10 folds (3 per fold); every window takes its subject's fold (subject-wise).

The remaining lines (helpers, guards, statistic and threshold line) are identical in the two files. `criterion` occupies
lines 96 to 121 of fixed_body.py.

Implementation choices the private note leaves open (identical in both files): Random forest: 50 trees, fully grown (minimum leaf 1), floor(sqrt(variables)) = 6 variables per node, Gini; window is walking when the mean leaf share > 0.5. Windows (faulty) or subjects (fixed) assigned to folds at random.

**Estimand.** Faulty: error on windows whose subject also appears in the fitting data. Fixed: error on windows of subjects never used in fitting.
