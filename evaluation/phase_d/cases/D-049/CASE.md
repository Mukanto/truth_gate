## 4. Mapping
Error as documented in the private note: SMOTE (k = 5 nearest minority neighbours, minority synthesised up to the majority count) is applied to all 600 records; stratified 5-fold cross-validation with a CART decision tree (max depth 6) then runs on the enlarged data, and the F1 score of the positive class, pooled over the test folds (which contain synthetic rows), is the statistic. Claim holds when F1 >= 0.50.

Correction as documented: Stratified 5-fold cross-validation on the original 600 records; SMOTE is applied to the training folds only; F1 of the positive class is computed on the original (non-synthetic) test-fold records, pooled.

Model-family choice in the private note: CART decision tree (max depth 6) with SMOTE (k = 5). Chosen by me: the abstract names no classifier; a tree makes the synthetic-neighbour overlap visible and is cheap.

Lines of `faulty_body.py` that carry the error (parameters are those of the private note, where the source gives none the brief author's choice; the source's own sizes and bias figures are in the private note's `sizes_note`):
   - faulty_body.py line 142, `xs, ys = _smote(x, y, rng, 5)`: SMOTE (k = 5) applied to all records before any fold is formed.
   - faulty_body.py line 143, `folds = _stratified_folds(rng, ys, 5)`: folds drawn on the enlarged data, so synthetic rows are test rows.
   - faulty_body.py line 144, `ye = ys`: F1 is scored on the enlarged labels.
   - faulty_body.py line 148, `pred[te] = _tree_scores(xs[~te], ys[~te].astype(float), xs[te], rng, 6, 1) > 0.5`: CART depth 6 trained and tested on enlarged folds.

Lines of `fixed_body.py` that carry the correction:
   - fixed_body.py line 142, `folds = _stratified_folds(rng, y, 5)`: folds drawn on the original records.
   - fixed_body.py line 147, `xs, ys = _smote(x[~te], y[~te], rng, 5)`: SMOTE (k = 5) on the training part of each fold only.
   - fixed_body.py line 143, `ye = y`: scored on the original test-fold records.

The remaining lines (helpers, guards, statistic and threshold line) are identical in the two files. `criterion` occupies
lines 128 to 149 of fixed_body.py.

Implementation choices the private note leaves open (identical in both files): Stratified folds (by label). SMOTE: minority = smaller class, nearest neighbours by Euclidean distance on the raw variables, synthetic count = difference of class sizes, base row, neighbour and gap drawn uniformly at random; a class with fewer than 2 records cannot be interpolated and is left unchanged. CART: Gini (variance reduction on a 0/1 target), depth <= 6, minimum leaf 1, midpoint thresholds, positive when leaf share > 0.5.

**Estimand.** Faulty: F1 on a test set that contains synthetic interpolations of training minority rows. Fixed: F1 on original records of new data from the natural class distribution.
