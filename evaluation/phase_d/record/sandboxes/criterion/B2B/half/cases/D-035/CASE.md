## 4. Mapping
Error as documented in the private note: SMOTE (k = 5) is applied to all 1500 records; stratified 10-fold cross-validation with a 5-nearest-neighbour classifier then runs on the enlarged data; balanced accuracy (mean of sensitivity and specificity, pooled test-fold predictions including synthetic rows) is the statistic. Claim holds when balanced accuracy >= 0.65.

Correction as documented: Stratified 10-fold cross-validation on the original 1500 records; SMOTE on the training folds only; balanced accuracy on the original test-fold records, pooled.

Model-family choice in the private note: 5-nearest-neighbour classifier on standardised variables, with SMOTE (k = 5). Chosen by me: the abstract names no classifier; k-NN is where synthetic-neighbour overlap bites hardest, and it is cheap.
